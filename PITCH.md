# Pitch — Club de detective literario

A **3–4 minute** talk in simple English. Speak slowly (about 110–130 words per minute). If you go a little faster, it lasts about 3 minutes. If you pause to show the app, it lasts about 4 minutes.

---

## Script

Hello.  
My name is \[your name], and this is **Club de Detective Literario**.  
It is a reading game for kids from **8 to 15 years old**.

Many children do not like long pages of text. They get bored. They skip words. Then they cannot answer questions, and they stop reading.

Our idea is simple: **read like a detective, one word at a time.**

The student opens the app and chooses a name.  
Then they pick a group: **Junior** (8 to 11) or **Cadete** (12 to 15).  
Each group has different stories.

On the home screen they see a **train**.  
Each wagon is one case — a short text from a classic book or a local story.  
The first wagon is open. When they finish it, the next wagon opens.  
Our mascot is **Folio**, a little book that rides with them.

Inside a case, Folio shows the text **one word at a time**.  
The student can change the speed, from slow to very fast — up to **one thousand words per minute**.  
Every word is lined up on the **second letter**, in light red, so the eyes stay in the same place.  
If the word has only one letter, like “a” or “y”, that letter is in the center, also in red.

When the last word ends, they go **straight to three questions**.  
Each question has four answers. They have **two tries**.  
If they are fast **and** they understand the story, they get **more points**. Speed is a **multiplier**, not a small extra.  
If they read too slow, they still get points, but less.  
If they miss the reading, they lose the speed bonus.

At the end they see their score: correct answers, speed, a clean-case bonus, and a streak if they play every day.  
There is a **weekly ranking** for their age group, and **medals** for authors. Medals are for fun. They do not change the league.

This is a **prototype**. It is not a full school product yet.  
But in a few minutes a child can enter, ride the train, read at their own speed, solve a case, and see their name on the board.

**The message is:** reading can feel like a game, without losing the story.  
Thank you. I can show you the train and one case live.

## Timing

| Part | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Problem + idea | ~45 s |
| Train, Folio, Junior / Cadete | ~50 s |
| Reading (one word, ppm, red letter) | ~50 s |
| Questions and points | ~45 s |
| League, medals, close | ~40 s |

If you show the screen, say only: *“Here is the train. I open a wagon. I set the speed. The words appear. Then the questions start.”* and then continue the script.

**Keywords if people ask:** speed reading, one word at a time, points multiplier, weekly league, not production yet.
